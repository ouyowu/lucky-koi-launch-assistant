Privacy Policy

Lucky Koi Launch Assistant

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, how it is used, and how it is handled when using Lucky Koi Launch Assistant.

Effective Date: May 6, 2026

1. Overview

Lucky Koi Launch Assistant is a private internal tool used to manage Pinterest publishing workflows and digital product launch activities for my own business.

2. Information We May Access

Lucky Koi Launch Assistant may access limited information made available through authorized Pinterest API connections, including:

  • Account information related to the authorized Pinterest account
  • Pin content, board information, and publishing-related metadata
  • Technical authentication data required to maintain authorized API access

3. How Information Is Used

The information is used only for the following purposes:

  • Preparing and publishing Pins for my own digital products
  • Managing Pinterest content workflows
  • Organizing product launch materials and related publishing tasks
  • Maintaining authorized Pinterest API connections

4. Data Sharing

Lucky Koi Launch Assistant does not sell personal data.

Information accessed through Pinterest is not shared with third parties except when required to operate authorized Pinterest API functionality or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

Authorized account information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the tool, manage publishing workflows, and maintain account access.

If access is no longer needed, related stored tokens or connection data will be removed within a reasonable period.

6. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect authorized account information and API credentials from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

7. Third-Party Services

Lucky Koi Launch Assistant may rely on third-party platforms and services, including Pinterest and related website or hosting tools. Use of those services may also be subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Changes To This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the tool, its usage, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted at the same location with a revised effective date.
